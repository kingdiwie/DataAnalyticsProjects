Global AI Adoption & Workforce Impact


https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/dwayne.chama/viz/GlobalAIAdoptionWorkforceImpactFinal/Dashboard1


Goal : My goal for this project is to understand the implementation of AI in today's world. I go on to show why it is being implemented and also illustrating its impact on the labor workforce.

Data : Data set was downloaded from Kaggle, this dataset provides a structured view of how companies across industries and countries adopt AI technologies and how those decisions affect productivity, workforce dynamics and operational outcomes. It spans across a total of 150,000 companies for the year 2025.

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohankrishnathalla/global-ai-adoption-and-workforce-impact-dataset

Approach : I applied 4 different methods to answer the questions that I had as listed below;
1. Descriptive - This method explains what's happening
2. Diagnostic - This tells us why it's happening
3. Comparative - This shows how it differs across groups
4. Implication - What it means going forward 


Key Findings : I had found some interesting information once I finished understanding thee data;
1. The technology sector is the most leading with the highest adoption rate
2. Companies that actually implement AI into their journey see noticeably bigger productive gains.
3. Each sector implementing AI se an average of 4% margin in cost reduction and revenue growth showing the effectiveness and efficiency of these systems
4. Re-skilling is the biggest response to AI, it is changing what people's jobs look like

In a nutshell, AI adoption is really still early though companies that are implementing it are actually winning and they aren't just cutting costs but growing very well. Though unlike the elevator operator, most jobs today aren't disappearing because of AI but they are being reshaped. The masses are asking "is AI here to take our jobs" but the better question is, "How is my role about to change".
